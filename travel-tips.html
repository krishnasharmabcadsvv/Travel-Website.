Essential travel tips for a smooth and enjoyable trip across India!

Travel Tips

🧳 Packing Essentials

Carry light cotton clothes for summer, warm layers for winters, and comfortable footwear. Don't forget sunscreen, a water bottle, and a basic first-aid kit.

💰 Budget Travel Tips

Use Indian Railways for long distances, book accommodations in advance, and enjoy street food. Look for government-run tourist lodges for affordable stays.

🔒 Safety Tips

Beware of scams, use official transport, and keep emergency contacts handy. Always keep a photocopy of your ID documents when travelling.

🙏 Cultural Etiquette

Dress modestly in temples, remove footwear before entering homes and religious places, and greet with 'Namaste'. Always ask before taking photos of people.

🍽️ Food & Water

Drink only bottled or filtered water. Try local street food but choose busy stalls. Inform vendors of any food allergies before ordering.

🚌 Getting Around

Use apps like Ola and Rapido for city transport. For intercity travel, book train tickets on IRCTC well in advance, especially during holidays.

📱 Useful Apps

Download IRCTC for train bookings, Google Maps for navigation, and Practo or mFine for medical emergencies. A translation app is helpful in remote areas.

🌦️ Best Season to Travel

October to March is the best time to visit most parts of India. Avoid peak summer (May–June) in plains, but hills like Himachal are perfect then.
