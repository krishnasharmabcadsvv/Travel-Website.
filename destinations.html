Explore top Indian travel destinations with must-visit places, local tips, and cultural experiences!

Jaipur, Rajasthan

Best Time to Visit: October - March

Top Attractions:

  • Amber Fort
  • Hawa Mahal
  • City Palace

Local Food to Try: Dal Baati Churma, Ghewar

🏖️ Goa

Best Time to Visit: November – February

Top Attractions:

  • Baga Beach
  • Dudhsagar Waterfalls
  • Basilica of Bom Jesus

Local Food to Try: Goan Patal Bhaji, Bebinca

🕌 Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh

Best Time to Visit: October – March

Top Attractions:

  • Kashi Vishwanath Temple
  • Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat
  • Sarnath

Local Food to Try: Kachori Sabzi, Malaiyyo

👑 Mysore, Karnataka

Best Time to Visit: October – February

Top Attractions:

  • Mysore Palace
  • Chamundi Hills
  • Brindavan Gardens

Local Food to Try: Mysore Pak, Masala Dosa

🌴 Kerala

Best Time to Visit: September – March

Top Attractions:

  • Alleppey Backwaters
  • Munnar Tea Gardens
  • Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary

Local Food to Try: Appam with Stew, Kerala Prawn Curry

🏔️ Leh-Ladakh

Best Time to Visit: May – September

Top Attractions:

  • Pangong Lake
  • Nubra Valley
  • Thiksey Monastery

Local Food to Try: Thukpa, Momos
